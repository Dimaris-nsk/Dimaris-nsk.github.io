Thumbnail submission and review · Access by invitation

STATA Preview Intake

Known to its users as «Подача превью», this application lets invited team members submit and edit video thumbnails through their own Google accounts. It keeps the author's identity out of the shared review view until an authorized reviewer explicitly reveals it.

The application is being prepared and tested. This page is not a claim of Google verification.

AboutPrivacyTerms

About the application

Authors upload a PNG or JPEG thumbnail and enter a title and supporting information. Designated reviewers record their reviews. Approved application administrators maintain a private history of submissions, edits, reviews, and identity disclosure.

Selected submission fields and the image are copied to a shared review spreadsheet. Author names remain hidden until the required reviews have been recorded and an authorized user explicitly publishes the names. Hiding a name later cannot undo what people have already seen or erase Google's document history.

The application does not upload, edit, publish, or delete videos on YouTube.

Privacy policy

Information received from Google

The application uses your Google account's verified email address and unique account identifier to identify you, match you to the administrator's invitation, enforce your role, and attribute your actions in the private audit history. Your display name and application role are assigned by an administrator.

The sign-in component requests Google identity and email permissions, plus permission for Apps Script to connect to an external service. That connection sends authenticated application requests to the application's own processing component. It does not request permission to read your Gmail, your personal Drive files, or your YouTube statistics.

Information you submit

The application stores the image and text you choose to submit, including titles, source links, comments, dates, work identifiers, and review decisions. A source-file link entered in the private field is available to its author and application administrators; it is excluded from the shared review view. Avoid putting your name or other private information inside an image or a shared text field.

Storage and access

The application runs on Google Apps Script. Submissions, identity bindings, private audit records, images, and backups are held in administrator-controlled Google Sheets and Google Drive storage. Application administrators can see author identities and the audit history. Other invited users receive only the information permitted by their application role. The shared spreadsheet follows its own access list.

To recover an interrupted submission, the browser tab keeps pending-operation information, including its text fields, in session storage. The image itself is not saved there and must be selected again if needed.

Google hosts the application and its storage. This information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. See the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement for their handling of service and website data.

Limits on use and sharing

Data is used to operate the submission, review, publication, recovery, and audit functions described here. The application has no automatic AI-processing integration. For support requested by an affected user, or for security troubleshooting, the operator may use AI assistants such as Codex and Claude. Only the records necessary for that specific task may be shared, with the affected user's consent when required; this does not authorize general-purpose AI analysis of Google identity data. Those providers operate under their own terms and the operator's account settings; this notice makes no claim about their retention or model-training policies.

The application does not sell user data, train AI models on it, or use it for advertising or lending decisions. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion requests

Submissions and their history are retained in the application's working records and backups. The current application has no automatic record-expiry schedule and no self-service deletion button. Hiding an author's name changes its visibility, not the retained history.

You can revoke authorization through Google Account permissions. This prevents further authorized sign-in or requests unless access is granted again; it does not by itself erase previously submitted records, shared spreadsheet copies, or backups.

For a request to remove personal information or close application access, contact the administrator at admin@masterskaya.com.co, identifying the account and affected submissions. Such requests require administrator handling across the working records, audit history, spreadsheet copies, and backups.

Terms of use

  • Access is limited to individually invited accounts and their assigned roles. Use your own account and submit only material you are authorized to share for review.
  • A saved submission and a confirmed spreadsheet publication are different stages. Check the status displayed by the application before repeating an uncertain operation.
  • Identity disclosure and hiding require explicit actions by authorized users. Administrators retain the private history described in the privacy policy.
  • To stop using the application, revoke Google authorization and contact the administrator for any requested record removal.
